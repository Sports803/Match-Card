Where post links come from

On load the tool reads your Blogger feed at /feeds/posts/default and indexes every post title and permalink. Each fixture is then matched against that index on team names, so Espanyol vs Elche – La Liga Live Stream resolves to its real URL even though the slug ends in -live-stream and Brentford vs Chelsea's does not.

When there is no post yet

The row shows No post found. Use Search site on that row to run a live feed query for it, or paste the URL by hand. With Only publish matches that have a post on, unmatched fixtures are skipped rather than published with a dead link.

How matching scores

A post has to contain a token from both team names to be considered. Extra points for league words in the title, correct home-before-away order, and a publish date near kick-off. Anything below the confidence floor is treated as no match.

Keeping scores current

Sync scores re-reads ESPN for everything published and writes fresh scores and status back. Auto-sync repeats it every 60s.

Taking cards down

Remove finished clears anything that ended over three hours ago. Rows also have their own remove button.
